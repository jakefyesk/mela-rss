KFC Sauce

By Joshua Weissman

Ingredients

  • ½ cup (120g) mayonnaise
  • 2 Tbsp (30g) ketchup
  • 2 Tbsp (30g) honey
  • 2 Tbsp (30g) yellow mustard
  • 2 Tbsp (30g) Dijon mustard
  • 2 tsp (10mL) Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ tsp (1g) paprika
  • Pinch of cayenne pepper, *optional
  • ½ tsp (1g) garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp (<1g) onion powder
  • 2 tsp (10mL) white distilled vinegar
  • Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Add the mayonnaise, ketchup, honey, yellow mustard, Dijon, Worcestershire, paprika, cayenne (if using), garlic powder, onion powder, vinegar, and salt and black pepper to taste to a medium mixing bowl. Whisk until completely smooth and evenly combined.
  2. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving for the best flavor.
